# Child Safety Standards for Collecta

**Effective date:** 2026-05-27
**Last updated:** 2026-05-27

Collecta is a real-world photo collection app where users photograph physical objects and share their finds with other users. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform and to complying with the Google Play Families Policy and applicable child safety laws.

## 1. Prohibited content and conduct

The following are strictly prohibited on Collecta:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind.
- Sexualization of minors, including suggestive imagery, captions, or commentary involving anyone under 18.
- Grooming behavior — attempting to befriend, gain the trust of, or sexually solicit a minor through the app.
- Sharing personal contact information of minors, or soliciting personal information from minors.
- Content that depicts violence, exploitation, or abuse of children.

Any account or content involved in the above will be removed and the account banned, with no warning.

## 2. In-app reporting

Every find (photo + caption) and every user profile can be reported from inside the app:

- Tap the **⋯** (more) menu on any find or profile.
- Choose **Report**.
- Select a category (including "Child safety concern") and add an optional description.
- The report is delivered to our moderation team for review within 24 hours.

You do not need to leave the app to file a report. No account is required to be in good standing to report.

## 3. Moderation and response

- Reports are reviewed by our team within 24 hours.
- Content that violates our standards is removed immediately.
- Accounts engaged in CSAE behavior are permanently banned and their data preserved for law-enforcement requests.
- We cooperate with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and equivalent regional authorities where applicable. Confirmed CSAM is reported to NCMEC per US federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2258A).

## 4. AI verification

Photos submitted to Collecta are processed by Claude Vision (Anthropic API) for collection-matching analysis. Anthropic's safety filters automatically reject CSAM and return an error; such submissions never enter the public feed.
